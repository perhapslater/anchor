Need help connecting?

Connect this device to Tailscale and use Anchor's full HTTPS address printed by Tailscale Serve, including its port if shown. If that address cannot be reached, check your network access and the server's Serve configuration.

Tailscale uses the account connected on this device. Another browser profile does not change that identity. Ask your administrator to link your Tailscale email to an enabled Anchor account.
